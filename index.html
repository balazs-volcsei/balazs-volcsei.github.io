About my work

Software developer & mechanical engineer

I am a software developer and mechanical engineer with a background in complex systems and technical problem-solving. I design and build native Android applications from scratch using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, focusing on smooth user interfaces and reliable data systems that stay fast, secure, and fully functional even without an internet connection.

Coming from a metrology and quality engineering background, I approach software development with a strong emphasis on system stability, root-cause analysis, and structured testing. I am currently developing reactive, offline-first Android applications while serving as a Quality and Calibration Engineer at SK On Hungary.
